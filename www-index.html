218 Magnolia, not a highway billboard

A hole in the wall that smokes the ribs.

Halftime is a locally owned sports grill in Lincoln — about ten minutes up 78 from Pell City, on Magnolia Street South, not on the interstate frontage. Their Facebook says the quiet part: wings, burgers, fish. About 2,300 people follow the page. Dine-in, curbside, and in-store pickup. The website, until this one, was a black-and-yellow template with a stock mountain photo, invented menu prices, and hours that said “open daily.”

They are not open daily. Sunday through Tuesday the lights are off. Wednesday they post $1 Wing Wednesday. People who review it talk about smoke, a wait, and coming back through town on purpose.
