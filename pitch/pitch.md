## Objections

- **“I have Facebook.”** Facebook is where regulars see $1 wings. Google is where a Honda Odyssey from Pell City searches “wings Lincoln AL” at 5:40. The site is the phone number they can tap.
- **“We already have a website.”** The current one says open daily and shows a mountain. That looks closed or fake. Worse than none.
- **“Too much money.”** Basic package. No monthly if they hate subscriptions.
- **“We’re slammed Wednesday.”** That’s why the site has one action: call. No online ordering, no app.
- **“I don’t want to mess with photos.”** Fine — we launch on stock labeled as stand-in, then swap six phone pics when they text them.

## What not to say

- Don’t invent an owner first name.  
- Don’t quote 88¢ wings. Their graphic is **$1**.  
- Don’t say open daily or open Sunday.  
- Don’t reprint aggregator menu prices.  
- Don’t promise live music / cruise-in unless they confirm that week.  
- Don’t claim “#1 in Lincoln.” 4.5 / 113 is enough and it’s sourced.
